It all started with one question...

UFO sighting patterns may seem random or purely coincidental, but what if we could pinpoint the most likely locations and uncover the most common pattern characteristics? By transforming the dataset into meaningful visualizations, we aim to uncover and clearly communicate the insights behind UFO sighting trends.

Why USA?

The team decided to narrow our scope to the United States, where the majority of sightings occur, likely because the concept of UFOs is more culturally prominent and rooted in this region.

Timeline of UFO Sightings in the past 30 years

UFO sightings have been recorded since the 1940s, but for this project our team narrowed the scope to the past 20 years to focus on more relevant and consistently documented reports. From 1995 to 2010, UFO sightings remained relatively steady with only minor growth. Reports peaked in 2012, highlighted by notable U.S. cases such as a large triangular craft seen by an Air Force veteran at Langley AFB, a cylindrical object over Utah’s Skinwalker Ranch, and glowing orbs reported in Missouri and New York.

The peak may be linked to the official end of the U.S. government’s secretive UFO program, AATIP. Following 2012, sightings dropped noticeably, returning closer to the levels seen earlier in the decade.

Hmmm...What’s the most common shape?

Which shapes appear most often, and what might that reveal about the nature of these sightings?

UFO reports describe a wide variety of shapes, ranging from classic discs and spheres to triangles, cylinders, and even more unusual forms. With such diversity, it can be challenging to determine which shapes are reported most frequently. So, among hundreds or even thousands of reports, which shape emerges as the most common?

Click on the bars to learn about the top 5 UFO shape!

From the dataset, the most commonly identified ufo shapes are the light, triangle, circle, fireball and sphere shapes. We had intentionally excluded the unknown and other shapes as the credibility of those reports are up to debate as they lack detail in their sightings.

The light shape dominates UFO reports, with approximately 12,000 sightings, nearly double that of the other four shapes in the top five. UFO enthusiasts can therefore expect that a flashing light is the most commonly reported feature during sightings. However, the team remains cautious, as these flashing lights could also be mistaken for common objects such as aircraft or drones, so the result should be interpreted with a grain of salt.

Main Insight

California and Florida are two of the sighting hotspots in USA, with light-shaped UFOs being the most commonly reported. The average sighting lasts less than one minute.

When planning your next UFO-sighting trip in the United States, your best chances of observing activity are in California on the West Coast and Florida on the East Coast. Sightings most commonly report light-shaped objects, though triangular, circular, fireball, and spherical forms are also noted. Most encounters last less than a minute, though some rare cases extend beyond four minutes.

But also consider this...

Could some UFO sightings be something else...?

The team would like to note that people sometimes mistake UFO sightings for other objects, such as airplanes, clouds, and more. Even though the next sighting could occur just around the block from your home, there is always a possibility that it may be something else, as approximately 70% of reports are ultimately classified as false alarms. When observing a potential UFO, be sure to distinguish it from common objects before reporting the sighting. 70% of the report ended being a false alarm.
