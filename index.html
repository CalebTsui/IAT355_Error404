So...What is UFO?

First sighted in 1947, UFO, stands for Unidentified Flying Object, is any object or phenomenon observed in the sky that cannot be immediately identified. The term gained prominence after World War II with the advent of rocketry, leading some to speculate about extraterrestrial life visiting Earth.

Why the USA?

The team decided to narrow our scope to the United States, where the majority of sightings occur, likely because the concept of UFOs is more culturally prominent and rooted in this region.

For the UFO enthusiasts...

This website contains interactive visualizations that highlights when and where sightings most frequently occur, helping enthusiasts like you to identify meaningful patterns and make more informed prediction of UFO activity.

Insight

From 2005 to 2010, UFO sightings were fairly steady, with only a slight increase over time. In 2012, the number of reports peaked, which may be linked to the official end of the U.S. government’s secretive UFO program, AATIP. Following 2012, sightings dropped noticeably, returning closer to the levels seen earlier in the decade.

Insight

From the visualization, the eastern states reported more sightings than western states. Specifically, Chicago on the east coast and LA on the west coast reported to have the most reports within their respective regions. Our team’s hypothesis for this reason is that these cities are heavily populated, increasing the likelihood for ufo exposure. However, these cities are filled with lights and other atmospheric objects, making it plausible for some of these ufo sightings to be mistaken from something else.

Insight

From the dataset, the most commonly identified ufo shapes are the light, triangle, circle, fireball and sphere shapes. We had intentionally excluded the unknown and other shapes as the credibility of those reports are up to debate as they lack detail in their sightings. Our hypothesis for this reason is because these shapes are visually similarly to one another, making it likely that the most prominent would be visually similar shapes.

Main Insight

Based on the visualizations, Downtown Chicago is a sighting hotspot. Light-shaped UFOs have been reported there, and most sightings last less than one minute.

However, could it be something else...?

The team would like to note that people often mistake UFO sightings for other objects, such as airplanes, clouds, and more. Even though the next sighting could occur just around the block from your home, there is always a possibility that it may be something else. When observing a potential UFO, be sure to distinguish it from common objects before reporting the sighting.
